Privacy policy

Study Ladder · last updated 16 October 2026

Study Ladder helps students learn from their own notes through exam-style questions. This page explains what the app stores, why, and who it is shared with.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, used only to sign you in and keep your workspace private to you. We never see your Google password.
  • The study materials you upload. Files are read in your browser and only the extracted text is saved. Photos and scanned pages are sent to the AI to be read.
  • Your courses, lessons and answers, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • A daily count of AI requests, to keep usage within fair limits.

How it is used

Your materials and answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to write lessons and questions and to mark your answers. They are used only to provide the service to you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your workspace is private to your account. If you choose to share a course with the community, other signed-in students can see its title, learning path and exam format, plus the display name you chose. Your uploaded materials, answers and progress are never shared.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the site is hosted by Vercel. AI requests are processed by Anthropic.

Deleting your data

You can delete any course, and its materials, lessons and answers, from inside the app. To delete your whole account, contact the app owner at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen, and it will be removed.

Children

Study Ladder is for students aged 13 and over, or younger students with permission from a parent or guardian.
